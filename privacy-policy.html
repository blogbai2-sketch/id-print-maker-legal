Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ID Print Maker handles information when you use the application.

1. About the App

ID Print Maker helps users prepare ID cards, passport-style photographs and photo print layouts for physical printing.

2. Local Data and Photos

The app is designed primarily for on-device use. Photos, exported files and saved projects may be stored locally on your device or in locations you choose for saving or sharing. The app does not operate its own user-account system for its core features.

3. Firebase Analytics

ID Print Maker uses Firebase Analytics to understand app usage and improve the application. The implementation records events such as starting ID card or passport-photo flows, exporting PDF or JPG files, saving projects and opening projects. Firebase Analytics may also automatically process certain technical and usage information according to Google's SDK behavior and applicable settings. The app does not use Analytics to collect the contents of your photographs or the personal information printed on your documents.

Firebase Privacy and Security

4. Google AdMob

The app uses Google AdMob for banner, native and rewarded advertising. Advertising SDKs may process information needed for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and related advertising functions, subject to applicable privacy choices and Google's policies.

Google Privacy Policy

5. Google UMP and Consent

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable advertising consent and privacy choices. Where required, a consent message may be shown before advertising is requested. A Privacy Choices/Consent option is also available in Settings when required or available.

Google UMP documentation

6. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics, Google AdMob and Google UMP. These providers may process information under their own policies.

7. Use of Information

  • Provide printing, editing, saving and export features.
  • Understand app usage and improve the app.
  • Display and measure advertising.
  • Manage applicable consent and privacy choices.
  • Support security and reliable operation.

8. Data Sharing

ID Print Maker does not sell your photographs or saved project files. Information processed by integrated third-party SDKs may be processed by those providers as necessary for their services.

9. Retention and Deletion

Local files and projects remain on your device until you delete them or they are otherwise removed. Third-party providers retain information according to their own policies and settings.

10. Children's Privacy

ID Print Maker is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

11. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect the app and its local data environment. No software or device can be guaranteed completely secure.

12. Your Choices

You can manage applicable advertising privacy choices through the Privacy Choices/Consent option in Settings when available, and manage device permissions and local files through Android.

13. Changes

This policy may be updated when app features, integrated services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised date.

14. Contact

For privacy or support questions, please use the Contact Us option inside the ID Print Maker app.
